Privacy Policy

Admissara | Beta draft 0.10 (November 2026)

Status: Beta draft for legal review. Written to meet the principles of the EU and UK GDPR, Turkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698), the U.S. COPPA and state privacy laws (including the CCPA/CPRA), Canada's PIPEDA, and comparable laws in Asia.

1. Where your data is stored

Your account and profile data is stored on your own device, in the App's local storage. When you create an account or sign in with email, your email address, a protected (hashed) password, your name and account type, and a synced copy of the data you enter are also stored with our cloud service provider, Supabase, on servers in the United Kingdom (London). This is what makes activation emails, password reset and use on more than one device possible. Database rules allow each account to read only its own data; a counselor you have linked with an invite code can read (not change) your synced data until you remove the link.

2. What the App collects

Data you enter: name, email, date of birth, citizenship, residence, location and time zone, school, grade, test scores, activities, honors, essays, financial aid figures, and, for counselors, professional identity details. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Two-step verification secrets stay on your device.

3. Why it is used (purposes and legal bases)

To provide the features you request (performance of a contract), to show deadlines in your time zone, and to keep your account secure (legitimate interests). Where consent is required, including for minors, it is collected at sign-up and can be withdrawn by deleting the account.

4. Network requests

The App contacts: our cloud service provider (Supabase) for sign-in, activation and password-reset emails, syncing your data, counselor links and messages, and feedback you send; the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard API for federal college data (no personal data is sent); Google Fonts for typography; and a public code library when reading a PDF or Word resume. If the App meets an unexpected error, it sends a short technical report (the error text, the screen name and the App version, with no name, email or profile content) so the fault can be fixed. Links you open go to official third-party sites under their own privacy policies.

5. Children

No account may be created by anyone under 13. Users under the age of digital consent in their country need a parent or guardian's consent, recorded at sign-up. The App does not sell or share personal data, does not show advertising, and does not profile minors.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Settings, Export my data) and delete (Settings, Delete account) all of your data at any time. Deleting your account in the App removes the data on your device and erases your cloud account, synced data, counselor links and messages.

7. Retention

Data is kept until you delete it.

8. Security

Salted password hashing, optional time-based two-step verification (TOTP), and recovery codes. Protect your device with its own lock as well.

9. International transfers

Cloud data is stored in the United Kingdom. If you use the App from another country, your data is transferred there; our provider acts as a processor under its data processing terms.

10. Contact and controller

The data controller, its representative in the EU/UK where required, and its data protection contact will be named here before public launch.
