Privacy Policy

College Admissions Guide | Beta draft 0.9 (November 2026)

Status: Beta draft for legal review. Written to meet the principles of the EU and UK GDPR, Turkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698), the U.S. COPPA and state privacy laws (including the CCPA/CPRA), Canada's PIPEDA, and comparable laws in Asia.

1. Where your data is stored

In this beta, all account and profile data is stored only on your own device, in the App's local storage. It is not sent to us or to any server. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it deletes it.

2. What the App collects

Data you enter: name, email, date of birth, citizenship, residence, location and time zone, school, grade, test scores, activities, honors, essays, financial aid figures, and, for counselors, professional identity details. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Two-step verification secrets stay on your device.

3. Why it is used (purposes and legal bases)

To provide the features you request (performance of a contract), to show deadlines in your time zone, and to keep your account secure (legitimate interests). Where consent is required, including for minors, it is collected at sign-up and can be withdrawn by deleting the account.

4. Network requests

The App contacts external services only when you use a feature that needs them: the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard API for federal college data (no personal data is sent), Google Fonts for typography, and a public code library when reading a PDF or Word resume. Links you open go to official third-party sites under their own privacy policies.

5. Children

No account may be created by anyone under 13. Users under the age of digital consent in their country need a parent or guardian's consent, recorded at sign-up. The App does not sell or share personal data, does not show advertising, and does not profile minors.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Settings, Export my data) and delete (Settings, Delete account) all of your data at any time. Because data never leaves your device in this beta, these rights are exercised directly in the App.

7. Retention

Data is kept until you delete it.

8. Security

Salted password hashing, optional time-based two-step verification (TOTP), and recovery codes. Protect your device with its own lock as well.

9. International transfers

None in this beta, because data is not transmitted.

10. Contact and controller

The data controller, its representative in the EU/UK where required, and its data protection contact will be named here before public launch.
