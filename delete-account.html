Delete your account and data

College Admissions Guide | Beta draft 0.9 (November 2026)

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Your data, choose Delete account and confirm.

This removes your profile, lists and settings from the device immediately. When the cloud service is enabled, it also deletes your cloud account and synced data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Parents and guardians can delete a child's account from My children.

If you can no longer sign in, contact us through the support address listed on the app store page and we will delete the account after confirming you own it.
