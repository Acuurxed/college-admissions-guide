Children and Parental Consent Policy

College Admissions Guide | Beta draft 0.9 (November 2026)

Status: Beta draft for legal review.

Children under 13. A child under 13 cannot create an account. A parent or legal guardian (18 or older) must first create a parent account, give consent, and then create the child's account under "My children". The parent can view, export or delete the child's data, issue a new temporary password, or delete the account at any time. The consent date and the parent's account are recorded with the child's account. Once the cloud service is enabled, parent accounts will also be confirmed by email before a child account can be created, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and comparable laws.

Ages 13 and over. The App asks for date of birth and country of residence at sign-up and compares the age with that country's age of digital consent (for example 13 in the United States and the United Kingdom, 14 to 16 in many EU member states, 16 where a country sets no lower age). A conservative age of 18 is applied for Turkiye, India and Brazil, where parental authority rules for minors apply.

Below that age, a parent or legal guardian must enter their name and email and confirm consent. The App records the consent on the device. Guardians may review, export or delete the child's data in Settings at any time.

The library of programs only shows a program's age and eligibility requirements; it does not arrange travel, payments or contact with any program.
